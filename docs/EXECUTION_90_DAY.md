# 90-Day Execution Plan

Realistic, phased execution for the business infrastructure upgrade. No hype — focus on consistency and credibility.

---

## Weeks 1–2 — Launch and stabilize

- **Website**
  - Publish Insights section and all 5 articles. Share 1–2 on LinkedIn or email (no spam).
  - Confirm Free Cloud & AI Review landing page and form work; test submission and email (internal + confirmation).
  - Ensure nav and CTAs point to `/free-review` and `/insights` where intended.
- **Content**
  - No new long-form content. Fix any typos or broken links from the new pages.
- **Process**
  - Read through [Outbound Workflow](./OUTBOUND_WORKFLOW.md), [Demo Framework](./DEMO_FRAMEWORK.md), [Discovery Prep Template](./DISCOVERY_PREP_TEMPLATE.md). Add a simple tracker (spreadsheet) for outbound and for Free Review submissions.

---

## Weeks 3–6 — Inbound and outbound rhythm

- **Inbound**
  - Every Free Review submission gets: (1) confirmation email (already automated), (2) manual review within 1–2 days, (3) discovery prep doc filled from [Discovery Prep Template](./DISCOVERY_PREP_TEMPLATE.md), (4) custom demo outline from [Demo Framework](./DEMO_FRAMEWORK.md), (5) reply with next steps (e.g. schedule link).
  - If volume is low, use the time to improve the prep doc and demo outline templates from real submissions.
- **Outbound**
  - Start the 10 companies/week cadence. Even 5/week is fine at first. Log every contact and response.
  - No cold email automation. Personalize each message. Use drafts from scripts if helpful; send only after editing.
- **Content**
  - One new Insight article per month (or one refresh of an existing one). Topics: extend the five (e.g. “Part 2” on IAM or cost) or one net-new technical topic.
- **AI offerings**
  - No structural change. Optionally add one case study or “typical engagement” blurb if you have a non-confidential example.

---

## Weeks 7–10 — Consistency and first review

- **Pipeline**
  - Review pipeline: How many Free Review submissions? How many converted to calls? How many outbound replies? Adjust messaging or targeting if needed.
  - Demo framework: Use it on every discovery call. Refine slides 1–2 and 5–6 based on what prospects respond to.
- **Hiring**
  - If Stage 2 (Cloud/DevOps hire) is on the horizon, draft a short JD and clarify handoff (what founder keeps vs. what engineer owns). See [Engineer Hiring Plan](./ENGINEER_HIRING_PLAN.md).
- **Positioning**
  - Ensure all client-facing copy and calls use the same line: “Cloud + AI Engineering for Modern Infrastructure” and the four pillars (security-first, automation-first, AI integrated, cost-aware). No new positioning doc needed unless you’re changing message.

---

## Weeks 11–12 — Quarter close and next quarter

- **Metrics (internal, no fabrication)**
  - Free Review submissions, discovery calls held, proposals sent, deals closed (if any).
  - Outbound: companies contacted, replies, meetings. No “conversion rate” theater — raw numbers are enough.
- **Next 90 days**
  - Decide: more of the same (steady rhythm) or one big push (e.g. 2 Insight articles/month, or a small paid experiment). Prefer steady over bursts.
- **Docs**
  - Update [BUSINESS_INFRASTRUCTURE_SUMMARY.md](./BUSINESS_INFRASTRUCTURE_SUMMARY.md) with “what we did” and “what we’re doing next” so the next cycle is clear.

---

## What we are not doing (by design)

- No fake testimonials or inflated case studies.
- No automated cold email sending.
- No promise that “AI will fill the funnel.” Humans do outreach and discovery.
- No new product or service that we can’t deliver with current capacity.

---

## Realistic next steps after 90 days

- If inbound is steady: keep the same funnel; focus on conversion (prep doc, demo, follow-up).
- If outbound is working: keep 10/week (or 5); add a second channel (e.g. LinkedIn) only if you can sustain quality.
- If hiring is imminent: use [ENGINEER_HIRING_PLAN.md](./ENGINEER_HIRING_PLAN.md) to define the first role and responsibilities.
- If content is working: keep 1 article/month; consider one “pillar” piece (longer, link-worthy) per quarter.
