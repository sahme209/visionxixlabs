# Demo System — Reusable 8-Slide Framework

Use this structure for discovery calls and demos. Fill in per prospect using intake form data and research.

---

## Slide 1 — Their Stage

- **Purpose:** Show we understand where they are.
- **Content:** Company size, industry, current phase (e.g. scaling, optimizing, securing). One or two bullets from their intake (cloud provider, deployment method, main concerns).
- **Tone:** Factual, no flattery. “You’re at X; here’s how we’re going to make this session useful.”

---

## Slide 2 — Cloud Maturity

- **Purpose:** Align on current cloud posture.
- **Content:** Where they said they are (e.g. mostly manual, some automation, CI/CD in place). What “good” looks like for their size. Gaps we typically see at this stage.
- **Tone:** Neutral assessment. Not a report card — a shared baseline.

---

## Slide 3 — Security Gaps

- **Purpose:** Surface security without fear-mongering.
- **Content:** Common gaps at their maturity (e.g. IAM, network segmentation, logging). Tied to their stated “security concerns” from intake. What we’d look at in a deeper review.
- **Tone:** “These are the areas we’d focus on; we’re not saying you’re broken.”

---

## Slide 4 — DevOps Improvements

- **Purpose:** Connect automation to outcomes they care about.
- **Content:** Deployment method today vs. recommended next step. CI/CD, IaC, environments. One or two concrete improvements (e.g. “pipeline for service X”, “tagging and cost allocation”).
- **Tone:** Practical next steps, not a full transformation.

---

## Slide 5 — AI Opportunity

- **Purpose:** Position AI as part of infrastructure, not magic.
- **Content:** Their AI usage status (from intake). Where AI could fit: internal tools, workflow automation, secure integration. “Production AI, not demos” — we can help with deployment and governance.
- **Tone:** Opportunity, not pressure. Only emphasize if relevant to them.

---

## Slide 6 — Cost Governance

- **Purpose:** Show we think about cost and control.
- **Content:** Visibility (tagging, allocation), budgets and alerts, optimization levers. How this ties to their size and cloud spend.
- **Tone:** “So you can predict and control spend as you grow.”

---

## Slide 7 — Engagement Model

- **Purpose:** How we work and what they can expect.
- **Content:** Fixed-scope packages vs. ongoing; access model (we use their tools, no long-term access to prod); deliverables (runbooks, IaC, documentation). Reference [Cloud Review Session](/cloud-review) or [Solutions for Growing Teams](/solutions-for-growing-teams) as needed.
- **Tone:** Clear, no vague “partnership” language. Concrete next steps.

---

## Slide 8 — Next Steps

- **Purpose:** One clear CTA.
- **Content:** e.g. “Send us your intake form if you haven’t already” → “We’ll send a discovery prep and custom demo outline” → “Schedule a 30-min review call” → “From there, we can propose a fixed-scope engagement.”
- **Tone:** Simple, no pressure. They know exactly what happens next.

---

## Usage

- **Discovery call:** Use slides 1–2 to confirm stage and maturity; use 3–6 as talking points; close with 7–8.
- **Follow-up demo:** Full 8 slides, with their data filled in. Send a one-pager or short deck after the call if useful.
- **Templates:** Keep a master (e.g. Google Slides or Notion) with this structure; duplicate and fill per prospect. Do not send the same deck to everyone — personalize at least slides 1–2 and 5–6.

---
