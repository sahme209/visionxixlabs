## Authorized policy and boundaries

- NEW TEST subscription allowances use **monthly anniversaries, no rollover**. Monthly payment funds one monthly included pool; full annual collection funds twelve monthly windows, not one annual lump sum.
- Free→paid begins with the **full paid pool**. Earlier Free usage and already admitted Free holds remain charged to their original Free bucket even if their actual usage/settlement arrives after paid activation. Purchased-credit remaining balance survives the transition, with its existing validity/expiry unchanged.
- Public Free0/Pro29/Enterprise79 and original price/quantity/interval policy is already approved. No further economic or access approval is requested by this plan.
- Only explicitly enrolled new isolated TEST organizations may use V2. Existing calendar V1 intents/evidence/contracts retain their original semantics and immutable digests. V1→V2 reinterpretation or automatic backfill is forbidden. Legacy customers/support SOURCE/FLOW remain unchanged.
- Same authoritative billing event consumer and existing financial rows. No production Prisma model/migration, shared Prisma generation, public Checkout/webhook route, IO permit, live billing change or deployment. Fixture DDL is a proposed owned-loopback-only installation.
- Original create proof remains identity/attempt provenance, never collection proof. Existing full invoice/payment/charge/refund/dispute/current-read/revision/deadline/idempotency guards remain. No term changes, proration, discount, grace, writeoff or rollover policy is introduced.

## Versioned identity, anchor and window model

Use policy string `axiom_test_anniversary_v2` distinct from unchanged price catalog `axiom_public_test_v1`. A fixture-only enrollment is installed **before** the first V2 Free admission, bound to runtime/account/org. New prepared intents freeze `allowancePolicy` and enrollment digest in their durable body and canonical Checkout/session/subscription metadata; original request digest therefore binds them. Missing/different policy never upgrades a V1 intent.

Initial separately collected invoice creates immutable paid-basis evidence containing enrollment, original intent/session/customer/subscription, original create-proof digest, unchanged terms digest, first paid invoice evidence digest and original `billing_cycle_anchor`/first full service start. Verify current retrieved `Subscription.billing_cycle_anchor`, `start_date`, single item and initial service line against that anchor (installed SDK fields exist). Custom anchor configuration/trial/backdate/partial period remains unsupported unless its exact equivalence is proved; do not guess. No additional SDK request is needed just to read these existing subscription fields. Paid evidence V2 is a closed discriminated schema; V1 serialization/hash stays unchanged.

Define `F(n)` directly from original anchor UTC year/month/day/hour/minute/second, adding n calendar months and clamping day to the last valid day of the **target** month. Never iteratively add to the previously clamped date. Monthly service periods must equal `[F(n),F(n+1))`; annual periods `[F(12n),F(12n+12))`. Annual subwindows are the twelve adjacent `[F(12n+j),F(12n+j+1))`, j0…11. Require exact current invoice-line/subscription endpoints and nonnegative index; no future/grace authority. Examples:

- Oct18 14:15:16→Nov18 14:15:16; Nov1 does not reset.
- Jan31→Feb28→Mar31 (2027); Jan31→Feb29→Mar31 (2028).
- Feb29 2028 annual→Feb28 2029; twelve anchored windows, then the next annual proof funds the next twelve. Original day29 is restored in later months/leap years rather than drifting to day28.
- Exact end is excluded; UTC clock, not local timezone/DST. Annual window11 ends exactly at paid annual coverage end. Missing next collection denies at that boundary. Recovery in an already funded window does not grant another pool.

Bucket ID is canonical SHA256 of `{version:2,enrollmentDigest,basisDigest,kind,start,end}`. Paid basis excludes invoice/event ID from window identity so same-period replacement collection/refund recovery cannot mint a new pool. Free basis is the enrollment's original UTC-calendar policy, with IDs per UTC month; returning inactive in the same month reuses that original Free bucket. Included amounts derive only from public Free200 or original paid terms, never a stored independent credit grant.

## Minimal owned-fixture provenance storage proposal

Existing reservation columns cannot represent immutable admission attribution. Propose these sidecars in **fixture DDL only**, not a new financial ledger:

1. `NewTestAllowanceBasis(digest PK, org, runtime, account, kind, body jsonb)` stores immutable policy/anchor/identity provenance, **no balance or credit amount**. Free basis exists before usage; paid basis is inserted with verified initial binding/evidence in the same consumer transaction.
2. `OrganizationTestAllowanceEnrollment(org PK, runtime, account, freeBasisDigest, paidBasisDigest nullable, revision>0, body jsonb)` is an explicit version/scope marker and lock/fence. Policy/enrollment identity is immutable; paidBasisDigest can be set once; revision increments on that transition. Coverage retains its existing independent revision. No payment status is inferred from this marker. A never-paid enrolled Free branch additionally requires no paid basis, no contract and no legacy billing identity. Once a paid basis exists, missing/broken contract or coverage is Unavailable; only the existing originally-collected, verified-inactive policy can select Free. Negative active/unsupported evidence never falls back to Free.
3. `NewTestReservationAttribution(reservationId PK, org, enrollmentDigest, body jsonb)` references the existing reservation and stores immutable bucket/anchor/funding ceilings. It does not store spend or a balance. Add a separate write-once nullable `releaseEvidence jsonb` column for a validated definitive-unused verdict; it never changes immutable allocation body/digest and is committed with an allowed release. Body fields: `{version:2, policy, runtimeId,accountId,organizationId,enrollmentDigest,basisDigest,bucketId,kind,windowStart,windowEnd,admittedAt,allocation:[{source:'included',bucketId,maxCents}|{source:'purchase',purchaseId,maxCents}],digest}`. Sum ceilings must equal existing `reservedCents`; scope must equal the referenced reservation/purchases. FK/owned checks/triggers reject replacement/deletion and cross-org links. Fixture indexes/trigger checks on existing rows are owned DDL, no Prisma generation.

The sidecar is allocation provenance for **one existing hold**, not a duplicate usage/credit/hold ledger. Money/status remain authoritative only in UsageEvent, AIUsageReservation and AddOnPurchase. Without this sidecar (or an approved reservation schema change, currently excluded) the before-purchase/after-purchase race cannot be solved honestly. Root reviews this interface before code.

`UsageEvent.metadata.axiomTestAllocationV2` is reserved server-owned JSON. It contains `{version:2,reservationId,attributionDigest,enrollmentDigest,basisDigest,bucketId,kind,windowStart,windowEnd,admittedAt,state:'pending'|'settled',allocation?:[{source,bucketId?|purchaseId?,cents}]}`. The closed union forbids `allocation` on pending records and requires it on settled records. Pending records link to the original reservation; actual allocation amounts are sealed only on authoritative settlement and sum to `billedCents`. Preserve all unrelated metadata (including Gateway costSource). Never trust a request/body/general `input.metadata` value for this namespace. Existing unique externalUsageId and reservation.usageEventId are checked for exact org/provider/attribution; foreign or contradictory upserts deny. An owned partial uniqueness/immutability guard prevents two usage events for one attributed reservation and final attribution rewrites.

## Accounting and races

**Known facts only.** Explicit V2 enrollment requires no unclassified existing AI usage/holds. Existing purchase rows with no earlier usage/holds can be read from their real net ledger; otherwise missing historical allocation makes enrollment/read/admission Unavailable. After enrollment, every AI debit/live or historical hold must have valid scope and V2 provenance; a count/shape mismatch fails closed, including unattributed events from an uninstrumented writer. Do not silently ignore zero/pending rows or infer a bucket from settlement time, correlationId, current plan, createdAt, v1 payment evidence or a request-supplied label.

Pending usage is counted through its linked live hold only; settled usage through its sealed actual allocation only, so no pending/settled transition is counted twice. Included balance = current bucket allowance minus settled included allocations for that bucket minus active included hold ceilings for that bucket. Unused included credit does not move to another bucket. Purchased availability per `purchaseId` = current net delivered-minus-reversed minus **all** settled purchase allocations minus active hold ceilings across **all** Free/paid windows. Keep current_month validity at its recorded UTC month and permanent validity unchanged; transition neither expires nor reissues a purchase. Failed/pending statuses and partial refunds retain existing semantics; historical debits are never erased. Negative available clamps to0 and refuses new spend, not history rewriting.

Reserve included credit first, then eligible purchases by earliest existing expiry, createdAt,id; record that deterministic funding ceiling once. Settlement allocates authoritative billed cents in that same order up to reserved ceilings, releases unused capacity through existing statuses, and never exceeds existing reservedCents. Provider cost remains separate from capped customer debit. No credit amount is minted by the provenance tables. Reads validate bounded complete sets (max4096 usage/hold rows and100 eligible purchases for this inactive fixture slice; exceeding bounds→Unavailable, never a truncated grant).

Admission locks enrollment, reads coverage/plan and financial facts Serializable, then takes row locks on selected AddOnPurchase IDs in stable ID order and revalidates their actual current values. It atomically inserts the existing hold plus attribution only after the purchase and DB-time fence below. Serializable enrollment locking alone is insufficient: the existing refund writer does not take that enrollment lock. The final SQL must fence enrollment revision, coverage revision/event token, immutable basis/bucket IDs and DB current time inside both coverage and selected window. Initial paid binding takes the same enrollment lock and sets paid basis in the consumer's event/evidence/projection/intent transaction. A Free preflight that loses to paid transition cannot insert. A Free hold that committed first retains its original attribution. Paid activation grants the full new paid included pool regardless of Free usage; prior purchased allocations/holds continue reducing the same purchase balances.

**Mandatory race example:** Free hold100 admitted Oct17; paid activates Oct18; actual80 settles Oct19. The usage event retains Oct's original Free bucket, not the paid bucket. Paid included pool remains3000 for3Pro seats. If the hold instead reserved100 from a500 purchase, settlement leaves420 purchased credit and the full3000 paid included pool. A concurrent earlier pending purchase hold is never forgotten. The old request key returns historical progress only and cannot dispatch a new paid request.

Direct usage+attachment+settlement for V2 is one transaction against the admitted hold. Gateway pending event/attachment retains the snapshot; authoritative reconciler later settles to the same bucket even across purchase/window/cancellation. A failed/unknown accounting commit retains the existing unresolved hold; do not release it merely because the current bucket changed. Refund/dispute negative coverage still denies **new** paid authority; finishing an already admitted usage record does not reinterpret current status as permission.

## Financial architecture review amendments (required before Task2+)

The pure Task1 candidate module does not wire any of these IO interfaces. Both amendments below are safety requirements, not new economic-policy questions.

### Durable pre-dispatch unknown outcome

Actual current sources have a five-minute unlinked-reserved expiry (`aiCreditReservation.ts:54–56`), call the provider at `generate/route.ts:150` before attaching usage (`184–190`), and release on generic provider/usage failures (`163,196,203,210,214,218`). Therefore immutable bucket metadata alone cannot preserve a purchase-funded hold through a crash before usage linkage. The V2 branch must not inherit those release/TTL paths.

Use the existing string `AIUsageReservation.status` with a new V2-only closed state **`dispatch_unknown`**, no Prisma migration. Flow:

1. Admission inserts `reserved` plus immutable attribution. Only this not-yet-claimed phase may expire at its original TTL. Before expiration/release, lock/recheck the actual existing hold; an update where status=reserved must lose to a committed dispatch claim.
2. Immediately before any possible billable work, run `claimNewTestBillableDispatch(tx,reservationId)`. Lock existing hold, enrollment/coverage authority and its selected purchase rows; repeat current exact bucket/coverage/purchase/time checks, then CAS `reserved` with usageEventId=null and unexpired TTL → `dispatch_unknown`, **commit before** calling the provider. A late preflight or changed/refunded/expired funding cannot claim. A refused claim is definitive local non-dispatch and may release its still-reserved hold atomically; no provider call occurs.
3. A crash after that commit, before/after the first byte or before usage attachment, leaves durable **unknown** permission outcome. Count its included allocation in the original bucket and its purchased allocation globally, regardless of expiresAt, age, billing transition or missing usageEventId. Do not synthesize a usage event just to obtain metadata. No timeout, missing provider usage, generic throw, failed attachment/write/settlement or lost callback proves unused.
4. Known Gateway usage attaches as `pending_settlement` in the same transaction as the real pending UsageEvent. Direct authoritative usage records and settles from dispatch_unknown atomically. Late authoritative cost always uses the immutable original allocation. Existing Gateway cost cap and source ceilings remain. Conflicting/unknown writes retain the hold.
5. `releaseNewTestUnusedDispatch(tx,id,DefinitiveUnusedVerdict)` is the sole V2 release transition after claim. It requires a closed server-produced, operation/identity-bound non-acceptance proof, retained once in sidecar.releaseEvidence in the same tx. No raw exception string/request metadata is proof. Local isolated-provider denial is checked before claim and can release a reserved hold; it does not require a fake provider response. Provider-specific post-claim nonacceptance remains unsupported until independently proved, so ordinary provider errors conservatively retain dispatch_unknown.
6. Fixture constraints reject dispatch_unknown→expired/cancelled/released without validated write-once unused evidence; unknown→pending/settled requires the matching real usage link and allocation validation. V2 reads/expiry/release paths branch before legacy bulk expiry or generic release. Legacy statuses/callers remain unchanged. Historical idempotency returns progress only; no second dispatch or fresh bucket claim.

This fixes a concrete lifetime gap on the existing financial hold, not a second hold ledger. There is deliberately no automatic unknown-outcome garbage collection or grace/writeoff policy.

### Current purchase lock and final DB-clock fence

`aiCreditFulfillment.ts:98–130` updates AddOnPurchase refund/reversal/status in its own transaction without the proposed enrollment lock. A V2 preflight can also cross Oct31→Nov1 while its paid Oct18–Nov18 included bucket remains current. Therefore both admission and pre-dispatch claim need a **selected-purchase fence**, not just a bucket/month or Serializable assertion.

Lock selected actual purchase rows `FOR UPDATE` by IDs+organization in stable order. The existing refund/fulfillment `UPDATE` takes the conflicting PostgreSQL row lock automatically; no legacy refund semantic change is required. After obtaining locks, require the exact captured purchase fingerprint (org,status,deliveredAICreditsCents,reversedAICreditsCents,validFor,periodMonth,refundedAt) and recompute complete global purchase spend+holds including this admission as appropriate. Any changed snapshot/serialization conflict refuses and retries from fresh facts, never proceeds on cached net value.

Use a materialized locking CTE for exact IDs/row versions; **after its lock acquisition**, the final conditional INSERT/CAS must check all selected rows present, current net available covers the recorded ceiling, status is paid/partially_refunded, permanent validity/refund flags match, and each current_month `periodMonth == to_char(clock_timestamp() AT TIME ZONE 'UTC','YYYY-MM')`. A time predicate evaluated only before a lock wait is insufficient. The final time checks run after all authority/purchase locks, alongside exact coverage/bucket bounds and enrollment+coverage revisions. Repeat them at dispatch claim because purchase validity/reversal may change between reserve and billable work.

If refund wins first, row version/current fields force admission/claim refusal. If reservation wins first, the refund writer waits for its commit; later refund cannot erase that prior financial hold/attribution and future availability floors0. If reversal wins after reserve but before claim, the claim refuses without provider work and releases only a definitively unused still-reserved hold. If reversal occurs after dispatch claim, retain the original unknown hold/cost; do not issue replacement credit. Remote/provider state after the last committed local fence remains a residual race, not transactional remote authority.

Required RED race cases: (a) purchase-funded hold unlinked past five minutes after committed dispatch claim + competing admission; (b) crash before/after provider send followed by late authoritative usage and duplicate key; (c) generic provider/attachment/usage failure cannot release; (d) Oct31 preflight blocked on row lock, Nov1 final INSERT/claim with same paid bucket rejects expired current_month funding; (e) actual independent refund writer commits before lock or waits behind reservation/claim, with conservation in both orders. For deterministic SQL-time rollover, only an owned internal test clock-expression seam may be proposed/reviewed; normal SQL remains clock_timestamp, no ambient/request runtime clock override. Do not claim the race proved by a pure-clock test alone.
