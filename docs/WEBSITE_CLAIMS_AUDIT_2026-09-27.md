Last updated: 2026-09-27

## Central answer

No. The current downloadable application does not yet deliver everything required by the original vision. The website now states that more honestly. A real v0.1.7 multi-platform release exists, and important application-side workflow, safety, evidence, and connector modules exist, but provider execution, complete deployment journeys, installed-package acceptance, identity/credential recovery, retention/restore, and the master-spec scenario A–L verification remain partial, unverified, or blocked.
