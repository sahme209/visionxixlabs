Existing grants were consumed exact artifact invocations and explicitly limited writes to work. This proposed control adds a literal device-data write permission in one diagnostic child. The standing validation-first and execution restrictions require an explicit decision before implementation/execution of that changed experimental boundary. Conditional preparation/review/run may be approved together to avoid separate procedural checkpoints; no run until fixed review passes.
