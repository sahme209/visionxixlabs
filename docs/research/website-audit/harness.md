## Concrete original Axiom information architecture

Proposed, not implemented or approved as final: **Home → Govern a release → Review the exact change → Verify the outcome → Recover safely**. The workflow page should show the requester, target, required independent review and current readiness before its next-action link. Each step should link to the relevant versioned task guide and real evidence. Support, Integrations and Security/Trust remain directly discoverable rather than buried inside the release story.

Use a clearly labeled local/preview demonstration until authentic supported execution is captured. Show blocked, expired and uncertain outcomes alongside success; do not turn a marketing autonomy selector into real authority. The reference suggests useful workflow organization, not a reason to reproduce its broad product catalog, autonomous-operation claims, visual identity or performance figures.
