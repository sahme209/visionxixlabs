## Direction: a calm engineering instrument

Make Axiom immediately understandable as a workspace for deployment decisions. Use generous hierarchy, precise type, a quiet canvas and one strong product illustration at a time. The original space language should explain relationships: repository and target objects, an explicit human-review boundary, a directed deployment path and recorded outcomes. Preserve the owner’s preferred luminous blue/white edges and sparse particles as supporting material; avoid a full-page wallpaper, fake live statistics, perpetual neon effects or black-hole metaphors for customer data.

Apple influence means consistent controls, restrained depth, legible text, clear focus and predictable movement. It does not require copying an Apple layout. Cursor/Claude/Codex/Grok references contribute clarity and conversational focus, while Axiom keeps its own deployment vocabulary and honest states.
