## Original Axiom decisions

Use task-led grouping rather than a large undifferentiated product list. Keep doc product navigation separate from account/AI actions. Make unsupported or unavailable states explicit; do not silently replace a failed documentation link with invented information. Present on-prem/offline requirements and licensing as actual qualified capabilities only after A9 evidence, not merely because a competitor lists self-managed features. Preserve honest demo classes and accessible disclosures; a focused button without visible explanatory content is not a successful interaction test.
