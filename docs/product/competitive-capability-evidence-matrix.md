# Competitive capability evidence — Harness, Octopus and Axiom

Owner reaffirmed November 13, 2026: comprehensive competitive coverage, with a distinctly better Axiom experience. This is an acceptance obligation, not a claim of current parity or superiority. Security, premium clarity and ease of use apply to every row.

## Three required passes

1. **Documented capability:** verify the exact vendor detail page, availability, edition/tier, limitations and date. Separate shipped, preview and announced features.
2. **Axiom implementation:** link actual reachable source, authoritative records, negative tests and current status. A plan, port, mock or matching feature name does not prove equivalence.
3. **Comparable acceptance:** demonstrate the complete same customer task, including refusal, uncertainty and recovery; measure outcome, usability, security and cost under disclosed comparable conditions.

No row is complete until all three passes have evidence. Missing evidence means **unverified**, not absent. Intentional differences require explicit rationale; scope additions requiring owner decisions retain that gate. No automatic adoption, integration, cloud access or spending follows.

## What “better” must prove

Use the same disclosed task and environment to compare: time to first governed deployment; setup steps and completion rate; approval comprehension; unsafe-action refusal and bypass resistance; failure detection/recovery and duplicate-effect prevention; accessibility; latency; and actual AI/provider cost per successful outcome. Establish baselines before numerical claims. Independent review and repeatable evidence must support any superiority statement.
