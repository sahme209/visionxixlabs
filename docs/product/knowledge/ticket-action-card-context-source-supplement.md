# Ticket action cards: target and exact selection context

This supplement records the card-context change at source commit `006ad4e92a7e886dbd52f2df6616929f69698171`. It supplements the existing [Jira release controls](jira-release-controls.md) and [ServiceNow evidence and closure controls](servicenow-evidence-and-closure-controls.md); their approval, execution and recovery boundaries still apply. Association approval is provenance only and cannot approve a provider link or closure.

Each saved action card now heads its content with **Jira / ServiceNow · saved target · Deployment evidence link / Ticket closure · status**. The target comes from that action's saved review (`issueKey` for Jira, `issueNumber` for ServiceNow). A missing, non-string or blank target displays **Target unavailable**; the heading does not substitute the enclosing association's target or establish current provider state. Inspect the saved target/content, version, payload digest, deadline and recorded outcome before any decision. Source: `desktop/src/components/AssociatedTicketActionsPanel.tsx:58`.

Each article references its own heading with `aria-labelledby` and its record line with `aria-describedby`. The record identifies saved action ID, review version and attempt count. A component-level `useId` prefix plus the action ID supplies the heading/record IDs, including when the same saved action appears in separate mounted panels. These relationships describe the authored DOM; they are not native or assistive-technology acceptance evidence. Sources: `AssociatedTicketActionsPanel.tsx:12,58`.

**I reviewed this exact provider target and content** selects the tuple of action ID, version and payload digest. Selecting another card replaces that selection; changing the version/digest does not retain a matching check. Starting a panel request clears the selection. **Approve exact provider action** records the separate provider-action decision; **Send approved deployment link / Close approved ticket** deliberately dispatches through the existing action path. Their buttons require the matching selection and returned capability, and disable while busy, held or expired. The checkbox supplies no independent authority and cannot replace genuine eligible human review. Sources: `AssociatedTicketActionsPanel.tsx:29,38,58,60,63–64`.

The component schedules a local rerender at the earliest future action or closure-options deadline. An expired awaiting-approval/approved card displays its expiry warning and disables approval/dispatch without requiring another interaction or request. This UI clock is not a server eligibility check, an extension, a cancellation or permission to retry. Saved-outcome reads remain separate; UNKNOWN and consumed-attempt recovery continue to follow the linked guides. Sources: `AssociatedTicketActionsPanel.tsx:15–16,41–42,59,61,66,71`.
