## Phase 2: AI That Acts (2026–2027)

**Objective:** Prove that AI can **execute**—not just analyze. Ship autonomous remediation, recurring Axiom, and deploy-as-a-service. Convert analysis into action.

### 2.1 Autonomous Remediation Engine

**Today:** Axiom scores, suggests playbooks, exports configs. Humans execute.

**Target:** AI **executes** remediation. Connects to GitHub, AWS, Azure, GCP via OAuth. Reads current state, applies fixes, opens PRs, deploys with approval gates.

**Deliverables:**
- Connectors with **write capability** (GitHub PR, cloud API calls)
- Agent loop: observe → plan → act → verify → report
- Approval workflows: human-in-the-loop for prod; auto-apply for non-prod
- Audit trail for every action; rollback on failure

**Revenue:**
- **Outcome-based:** 15–25% of verified first-year cost savings
- **Remediation credits:** $25–$75 per autonomous fix applied (tiered)
- **Tiered automation:** Free = analysis only; Pro = 5 auto-fixes/mo; Growth = 25/mo; Enterprise = unlimited + rollback insurance

**Success metrics:** 1,000+ fixes applied in Year 1; 90%+ approval-to-merge rate; <5% rollback rate

---

### 2.2 Autonomous Website Deployment

**Today:** AI generates content, user reviews, human deploys or clicks deploy.

**Target:** AI **closes the loop**—generates site, provisions Vercel/Netlify/AWS, configures DNS, deploys, sends live URL. Zero human touch for qualified leads.

**Deliverables:**
- End-to-end deploy pipeline: generate → review (optional) → provision → deploy → notify
- Integration with Vercel, Netlify, AWS Amplify
- DNS automation (optional); SSL auto-provision
- Post-deploy: analytics hook, SEO baseline

**Revenue:**
- **Deploy fee:** $99–$299 one-time for fully autonomous site launch
- **Hosting rev share:** 5–10% of hosting referral (Vercel, etc.)
- **Managed tier:** AI keeps site updated; $49–$149/mo retainer

**Success metrics:** 500+ autonomous deploys in Year 1; <2% rollback; 30%+ upgrade to managed

---

### 2.3 Autonomous Sales & Qualification

**Today:** Forms, lead scoring, human follow-up.

**Target:** AI **qualifies and routes**—reads incoming requests, enriches with public data, scores fit, triggers personalized outreach (email/Slack), schedules calls, preps briefs.

**Deliverables:**
- Inbound → AI enrichment → fit score → routing rules
- Personalized email/Slack sequences (templates + AI variation)
- Calendar integration; brief generation for sales
- Feedback loop: conversion by nurture path

**Revenue:**
- **Per-qualified-lead fee:** $50–$150 per MQL that converts to meeting
- **Pipeline acceleration:** 3–5% of deal value for deals closed via AI-nurtured path
- **Outbound AI:** Optional; % of sourced pipeline

**Success metrics:** 2x meeting conversion from AI-nurtured vs manual; 20%+ of pipeline from AI-sourced

## Phase 3: AI That Operates (2028–2030)

**Objective:** Shift from **episodic action** to **continuous operation**. Infra runs with AI as operator. Platform fees and outcome-based revenue dominate.

### 3.1 Self-Healing Infrastructure

**Target:** Continuous observation of connected infra (AWS, Azure, GCP, K8s). AI detects drift, cost anomalies, security misconfigs, performance regressions—and **auto-remediates** within policy bounds. Rollback on failure. Weekly executive summary.

**Deliverables:**
- 24/7 observation; sub-hour detection for critical signals
- Policy engine: what can auto-apply vs. human-approval (per env, per resource type)
- Incident correlation: link remediation to avoided incidents
- SLA dashboard: uptime, cost trend, security posture

**Revenue:**
- **Platform fee:** $2K–$10K/mo per environment (tiered by complexity)
- **Savings share:** 10–20% of verified savings (cost, incident avoidance)
- **SLA-backed:** Guarantee X% incident reduction or credit (premium tier)

**Success metrics:** 99.5%+ detection accuracy; <1% false positive remediation; 95%+ customer retention

---

### 3.2 Autonomous Cloud Operator (Evolution)

**Today:** One-shot analysis, tier-gated outputs.

**Target:** **Recurring, autonomous** analysis. Connects to live infra. Runs daily. Detects drift, new risks, optimization opportunities. Auto-applies low-risk fixes. Escalates high-impact items. Board-ready reports auto-generated. No human needed to "run Axiom"—it runs itself.

**Deliverables:**
- Daily/weekly cadence; configurable per org
- Drift, cost, security, compliance in single run
- Auto-apply for low-risk (configurable); escalation for high-impact
- Board deck, executive summary, trend reports—auto-generated

**Revenue:**
- **Continuous Operator:** $500–$2K/mo per org (replaces one-off)
- **Enterprise:** Custom SLA, dedicated agent, white-glove onboarding ($5K+/mo)
- **Add-ons:** Connectors (AWS, Azure, GCP, K8s), compliance packs

**Success metrics:** 500+ orgs on continuous; 80%+ renewal; NRR >110%

---

### 3.3 AI-Powered Implementation Factory

**Target:** Customer describes goal ("migrate app X to EKS"). AI generates full plan, breaks into tasks, connects to repos/cloud, implements incrementally via PRs. Human reviews and merges. AI tracks completion, handles blockers, updates plan.

**Deliverables:**
- Goal parser: natural language → structured migration/implementation plan
- Task breakdown with dependencies; phase gates
- IaC and config generation per task; PR per phase
- Blocker detection; suggested resolutions; rollback path

**Revenue:**
- **Per-migration / per-project:** $5K–$50K outcome-based
- **Implementation credits:** Prepaid blocks ($10K, $25K, $50K); consumed per task
- **Enterprise:** Retainer for ongoing implementation support

**Success metrics:** 50+ migrations completed; 70%+ task completion without human intervention; <10% rollback

## Phase 4: AI That Anticipates (2031–2035)

**Objective:** **Zero-touch operations** for most scenarios. AI predicts, provisions, scales, patches, remediates. Humans for policy and edge cases. Platform becomes **autonomous operator**. Ecosystem and data flywheel create durable moat.

### 4.1 Zero-Touch Operations

**Target:** Infra runs itself. AI predicts capacity needs, cost spikes, security events. Provisions, scales, patches, remediates. Human only for policy changes and edge cases. **Platform as autonomous operator.**

**Deliverables:**
- Predictive models: capacity, cost, security, compliance
- Auto-provisioning and scaling within policy
- Patch and upgrade automation; vulnerability response
- Policy-as-code; human overrides; incident escalation

**Revenue:**
- **Revenue participation:** 1–3% of infra spend managed
- **Outcome guarantees:** Uptime SLA, cost cap, security posture—or refund
- **Enterprise:** Custom terms; dedicated infrastructure

**Success metrics:** 80%+ of routine ops automated; <5% human escalation rate; 99.9%+ SLA attainment

---

### 4.2 Agentic Sales & Revenue

**Target:** AI agents own segments of the funnel. Inbound leads get full AI nurture—content, demos, pricing, contract drafting. AI handles objections, schedules humans only when needed. **AI closes SMB deals autonomously.**

**Deliverables:**
- Full nurture automation: email, chat, video, docs
- Demo and pricing generation; contract drafting
- Objection handling; escalation triggers
- Closed-loop: deal outcome → model improvement

**Revenue:**
- **Rev share on closed deals:** 5–15% of first-year contract
- **AI-sourced pipeline:** Bonus on deals where AI did >80% of nurture
- **SMB self-serve:** AI closes; human optional

**Success metrics:** 30%+ of SMB deals closed without human; 2x pipeline efficiency

**Deliverables:**
- Anonymized telemetry; pattern extraction; playbook improvement
- Marketplace: third-party agents, playbooks, connectors
- Data products: benchmarks, industry reports, trend APIs
- Enterprise data licensing: custom benchmarks, compliance templates

**Revenue:**
- **Marketplace:** 20–30% of third-party revenue
- **Data products:** Subscription for benchmarks, reports, APIs

**Success metrics:** 100+ marketplace items; 50+ data product customers; 2x improvement in playbook effectiveness from flywheel

- **Cross-cloud** — One platform for AWS + Azure + GCP. Native tools are siloed.
- **Execution, not advice** — We apply fixes (PRs, configs, policies). They give recommendations.
- **AI that understands context** — Business goals, compliance needs, risk tolerance. Generic tools don't.
- **Outcome-based** — We charge on verified savings and results. Aligned incentives.

**Eight real-work solutions** (technical, not vaporware): drift detection & remediation, autonomous cost optimization, CI/CD gap auto-fix, security posture auto-hardening, compliance drift remediation, incident preemption, multi-cloud policy enforcement, autonomous migration assistance.
